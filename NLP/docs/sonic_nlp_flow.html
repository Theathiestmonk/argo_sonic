Events, step by step

    What the robot remembers between turns

    awaiting

    What the robot just asked (order_qty, deliver_done …). A bare “two” or “yes” goes to the node that asked.

    task

    The job to do on arrival: take_order or deliver. Survives a “did you mean…?” question; dropped if you change topic.

    table · location

    Where the robot is. Ordering never asks the table again after navigation.

    order · pending

    The cart. Kept when a wake word interrupts, cleared for a new customer.
